# guard sheet prompt

Use the supplied baseline image as the exact character identity source. Read
`../identity-lock.md` before generating.

Generate a 2x2 sheet of separate full-body game sprites.
cell 1: compact unarmed recoil, knees bent and one forearm protecting the torso; cell 2: agile unarmed defensive block, arms crossed in front of the chest. The two remaining cells must be fully transparent.

Keep the same source-facing direction, palette, clothing, facial features, weapon ownership,
apparent character scale, body centre, and foot/bottom baseline in every cell. Each sprite is
fully visible, centred in its equal cell, and surrounded by generous empty gutters. Use a perfectly flat,
opaque #ff00ff magenta background. Do not draw labels, borders, ground shadows, detached fragments, or
anything touching a cell edge. Do not change the character design between cells.
